# Changelog

## V2.1
- Summary is easier to read: Device, Health, Findings and Zips sections.
- Findings are grouped by where the problem is: kernel, ROM and apps, or battery and heat.
- Shows "No problems found" when everything looks fine.
- Root manager update support.

## V2
- Action saves kernel, ROM and crash logs as three zips.
- Saving keeps running if the screen turns off; one save at a time.
- GPU hangs and page faults are listed with the app that caused them.
- After a sudden restart, the restart reason is saved too.
